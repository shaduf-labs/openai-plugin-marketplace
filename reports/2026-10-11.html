A pending review is not a second-release queue

Scope: OpenAI public-plugin package review, with a bounded existing remote-MCP published-replacement branch. Account and review operations: NOT RUN. Independent read bracket 21:02:27–21:03:02 UTC—not exact HTTP, publication or rule-change times.

If a remote-MCP version is already published, keep it in place during replacement review and publish the approved replacement directly.
