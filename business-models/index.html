Developer guide · research checked 29 September 2026

Business constraints, not sales promises

Publication can provide a distribution route. It does not establish customer demand, a payment permission, enhanced placement or revenue.

Separate existing entitlement from a sales flow

Use an existing paid provider account
Permitted under the saved rules; still check actual provider identity and entitlement. It is not permission to sell or upsell inside the plugin.
Sell digital services, subscriptions, content or credits
Current plugin rules prohibit these sales/upsells through the plugin. Neutral entitlement explanations/informational links are distinct from upgrade or transaction links.
Physical-goods commerce
The permitted commerce category; external checkout is the standard requirement. Instant Checkout is beta for selected partners, not a general plugin capability.
Plugin payout and promotion
No universal plugin-sale payout or GPT Store-style revenue program was verified. Rules also prohibit ads/discovery manipulation; a public listing is not a paid promotion entitlement.

Two opportunity hypotheses to test

Our inference · no demand or revenue measurement

Existing SaaS: one authorized customer task

A provider might make a useful task available where existing customers ask for help. The external service remains its system of record; the plugin packages the authorized workflow.

Assumptions
Permission to integrate, reliable tools, compatible hosts and existing customer entitlement. The experience must be useful without an in-plugin digital upsell or promised discovery.
What would falsify it?
The task cannot be authorized safely; native/provider UI already serves it without meaningful added workflow value; users cannot reach it under the intended access rules; or the required commercial flow violates policy.
Evidence still needed
Lawful observed invocation, task-quality/failure records and actual intended-user feedback. No such demand study is claimed here.

Our inference · no measured time savings

Specialist or team: a maintained repeatable process

A narrow skill/package might reduce repeated setup when a tested procedure matters. Maintaining regression cases can make changes inspectable; it does not by itself prove better outcomes.

Assumptions
Genuine utility beyond a normal prompt, host/tool compatibility, accountable maintenance and an appropriate distribution channel.
What would falsify it?
The procedure adds no observable value over prompting, introduces unacceptable failures or depends on access unavailable to intended users. A listing or MIT source alone cannot rescue the hypothesis.
Evidence still needed
Compare outputs for the same bounded task, preserve failure cases and gather intended-user feedback before asserting value.

Commercial decision worksheet

  1. Existing relationship: who already has the provider account or authorized workflow, and what task do they need?
  2. Distinct utility: what output can they inspect that ordinary prompting or the provider UI does not already satisfy?
  3. Policy fit: does the proposed experience depend on prohibited digital sales, upsells, transaction links, ads or manipulated discovery?
  4. Channel: is local/team sharing enough, or does a public release add justified value?
  5. Maintenance: who owns source versions, provider access changes, regression tests and endpoint continuity?
  6. Missing evidence: which reliability/access/value assumptions still need real tests or feedback? Keep them labeled hypotheses.

This synthesis is not investment advice, researched market demand, an earnings forecast or a new payment capability. Publication and this guide establish no adoption, willingness to pay, placement or revenue.
